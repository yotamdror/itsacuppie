Ordering Info

Cuppies are $4.75 and there is a minimum of 6 per flavor. Gluten-Free cuppies are $5.25 each. We offer a 10% discount on orders of 2 dozen or more and a 15% discount on orders of 4 dozen or more. We require a minimum of 72 hours notice for all orders.

How To Order

E-mail (ItsACuppie@gmail.com) or call us (917-524-8025) to place an order. Once your order is placed, you will receive an invoice via PayPal with all of the details. You have the option to pay via PayPal, check made out to It’s A Cuppie, or cash on delivery/pick-up.

Custom Cuppies

Custom decorations/decals are available at an additional cost. Inquire for details.

Delivery/Pickup

We offer delivery anywhere in Manhattan, Brooklyn and Queens starting at $15. You can also arrange for a pick-up in Brooklyn Heights.

Shipping

We ship frost-your-own cuppies to the contiguous 48 states. You will receive frosting in a separate container so that the cuppies don’t arrive damaged. We ship all flavors except for whipped cream frosting, mascarpone whipped cream, marshmallow frosting, strawberry cupcakes, and blueberry cupcakes. Shipping rates start at $15. Inquire for details!

Cuppie Tower

Interested in renting one or all of our cupcake towers? We require a refundable $100 security deposit and charge the following:
  • $40 for small tower (holds about 30 cuppies)
  • $55 for 2 small towers (holds about 60 cuppies)
  • $70 for large tower (holds about 100 cuppies)
  • $90 for all 3 towers (holds about 160 cuppies)
