Sticky Toffee Cuppie

Molasses date cake with graham cracker inside topped with toffee frosting

Molasses date cake with graham cracker inside topped with toffee frosting

This cuppie flavor was inspired by one of our favorite desserts: sticky toffee pudding. What exactly is sticky toffee pudding? Pudding in England is just another word for dessert. To be technical, it’s a light cake made with dates and treacle (the British equivalent of molasses) that is served warm, drizzled (or drenched) with a hot toffee sauce and often served with ice cream or whipped cream. If you don’t like dates, never fear; the dates add some sweetness and moisture to the cake but otherwise you’d never know they were there!
